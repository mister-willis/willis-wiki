    Inspect It in Fusion: Teacher Notes

    Draft notes for fusion-60-design-for-printing. They go into the lesson plan once the lab is approved.

    Class Files Setup

    Upload the three STEP files to Class Files ▸ Design for Printing so they become Fusion designs with these names:

    File Contents
    THEY Orientations.step The 13 worksheet orientations (components T-A … Y-C) on a 250 × 230 mm Bed
    Hand Crank Print Plate.step Bracket, Crank, Axle, Knob in print orientation on a 140 × 90 mm Bed
    Hand Crank Assembly.step The same four parts assembled (axle through the bracket, crank pressed on, knob on the pin)

    Z is up in all three files, and the parts sit on Z = 0. The Bed is there so students can pick its top face as the pull direction, whether Fusion is set to Y-up or Z-up.

    Answer Key: THEY in Draft Analysis (direction: Origin Z, default ±2°, Tolerance Zone off)

    Green points up, red is a wall, and blue points down. Every face that sat on the Bed is blue once the Bed is hidden; the info box covers this.

    Orientation Blue faces not on the bed Verdict
    T-B Underside of the crossbar (sticks out 12 mm each side) Fails
    H-A Underside of the top leg (sticks out 16 mm each side) Fails
    H-B Underside of the crossbar, 10.000 mm between the legs Bridge, prints
    E-C Undersides of the middle and top arms Fails
    E-D Spine between the arms (8 mm bridges), plus the tip of the short middle arm, 4 mm above the bed Fails (floating tip)
    Y-B Outer undersides of both arms, 30° from vertical Prints
    Y-A The two faces inside the upside-down V, 30° from vertical Prints
    All others None Prints

    Answer Key: Hand Crank

    # Part Problem Rule Found with
    1 Bracket Bottom edges of the base filleted (R2) where they meet the bed: a blue strip that goes flat at the bed Edges Draft Analysis
    2 Bracket Top of the horizontal 6.2 mm axle hole: a curve that goes flat (hard to see in color; judged by the rule) Overhangs (teardrop it) Draft Analysis (reasoning)
    3 Bracket Ceiling of the tower opening: blue, 18.000 mm span Bridges Draft Analysis, Measure
    4 Bracket Four gussets 0.600 mm thick Walls Section Analysis, Measure
    5 Bracket Axle hole 6.200 on a 6.000 axle is a press fit (0.1); the axle has to turn, so it should be free (6.600) Fits Measure
    6 Crank Pin 2.500 mm, under 3 mm, and standing up, so the knob load snaps it across the layers Fits, Strength Measure
    7 Knob Hole 2.300 on a 2.500 pin: −0.1 per side, 12.06 mm³ interference; should be free (3.100) Fits Interference, Measure

    The two suspicious-but-correct features: - the Crank's 6.200 hole on the axle, a press fit, which is right because the crank must not slip on the axle - the Axle's 1 mm flat, which lets it print lying down with no overhang past about 42°

    Also correct, if a student asks: the rounded vertical corners and the chamfered top edges of the base.

    Fits row Shaft Hole Gap per side Is Should be
    Axle in Bracket 6.000 6.200 0.100 Press Free
    Axle in Crank 6.000 6.200 0.100 Press Press
    Pin in Knob 2.500 2.300 −0.100 Interference Free

    All of these numbers were checked on the exported STEP geometry, not the design intent: minimum distances, overlap volumes, circle diameters, gusset face spacing, and a face-by-face emulation of the draft analysis.

    Checked in Your Fusion (2026-09-23)

    Every step from 7 to 33 was run in Fusion 2704.1.53 on your Mac, using the three STEP files imported into unsaved designs. The Class Files copy steps (4–6) weren't run, because the files aren't uploaded yet.

    Step Result
    7 The Browser shows T-A:1 … Y-C:1 (the lab now mentions the :1)
    8 Inspect menu: Measure (shortcut I), Interference, Draft Analysis, Section Analysis
    9 A selection box with Body active picked all 13 letters and skipped the Bed
    10 Origin ▸ Z worked as the direction; OK is disabled until then. Clicks on Bed faces never register
    11 Tolerance Zone unchecks cleanly: green up, red walls, blue down. Fusion remembers the setting next time
    12 Blue shows only when you look up at a face. From a shallow angle, down-facing faces look gray or black; from about 60° below they're clearly blue. The lab has a warning box about this
    13 H-B's legs are 10.000 mm apart. Y-B's arm underside against the stem side reads 150°, so the lab's "subtract from 180°" is needed
    15 The analysis is named Draft1 in the Analysis folder
    18 The bottom-edge fillet and the opening ceiling are blue. The top of the 6.2 mm hole can't really be seen from below, so the lab has students judge it with the curved-face rule. The Axle's lower curve is also blue but meets its flat at about 42°; the color-box wording now allows for that
    19 The opening is 18.000 mm
    20 An active draft analysis stops the section cut from showing. Hiding Draft1 is now marked as required
    21–22 Clicking the base's front face was unreliable (it hit the bottom face). The lab now uses Origin ▸ XZ, which cuts the two front gussets, then Flip
    23 The gussets are 0.600 mm
    24 The flipped section also hides the Axle and Knob, so the lab now says why to hide it
    25 Measure has Precision (0.123 here). Diameters display two decimals (6.20 mm), and the step now says so
    26–27 Clicking a hole's or pin's curved face reports Diameter directly (Axle 6.00, hole 6.20); clicking an end edge often grabs the end face instead. The steps now use the curved face
    31–33 Interference Results: 1 group, Crank:1 / Knob:1, 12.064 mm³. The columns are Groups, Volume, Component 1, and Component 2. Fusion zooms in and shows the overlap in red
