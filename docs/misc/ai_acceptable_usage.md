### AI Acceptable Usage Scale

| **Level** | **Usage Category**           | **Description** |
|-----------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **0**     | **NO AI**                    | The assignment is completed entirely without AI assistance. Students rely solely on their knowledge, understanding, and skills. AI must not be used at any point during the assignment. |
| **1**     | **IDEAS AND STRUCTURE**       | AI can be used for brainstorming, creating structures, and generating ideas for improving work. No AI content is allowed in the final submission. |
| **2**     | **EDITING**                   | AI can improve the clarity or quality of student-created work but cannot generate new content. Original work with no AI content must be provided in an appendix. |
| **3**     | **AI + HUMAN EVALUATION**     | AI is used to complete certain elements of the task, with students critically evaluating and discussing AI-generated content. AI-created content must be cited. |
| **4**     | **FULL AI**                   | AI is used as a 'co-pilot' throughout the assignment, allowing for collaboration with AI to enhance creativity. No need to specify which content is AI-generated. |
