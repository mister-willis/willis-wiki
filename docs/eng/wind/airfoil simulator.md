# Airfoil Simulator

## Overview

The [NASA Glenn Research Center](https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/) publishes [multiple aeronautics simulators](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/bga-simulations/) available for free use by the public.

One simulator useful to understanding airfoils is the FoilSim Student Airfoil Interactive simulator ([local](assets/FoilSimStudent/FoilSimStudent.html)) ([NASA](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/foilsimstudent/))

With this simulator you can investigate how an aircraft wing produces lift and drag by changing the values of different factors that affect lift and the factors that affect drag.
